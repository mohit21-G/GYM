PROMPT 09 — FOOD LOGGING BUSINESS LOGIC

Implement complete food logging business logic.

Flow:
Text
→ AI intent/entity extraction
→ validation
→ food master lookup
→ quantity/unit normalization
→ nutrition calculation from database master
→ food log creation
→ structured response

Requirements:
1. AI must not invent nutrition.
2. Backend must resolve food names against food master.
3. Support quantity and units.
4. Support meal type.
5. Store historical nutrition snapshot in food_logs.
6. If food cannot be confidently resolved, ask clarification.
7. If quantity is missing and required, ask clarification.
8. Support multilingual food descriptions.
9. Support update/delete through intent routing.
10. Calculate totals from authoritative database values.
11. Return useful UI-ready response.

Example:
3 khapli rotli khai

The system should resolve Khapli Roti from the food master and calculate nutrition based on configured serving data.

Add unit tests for common scenarios.
