PROMPT 10 — ACTIVITY LOGGING BUSINESS LOGIC

Implement activity logging.

Supported examples:
Walking
Running
Cycling
Swimming
Strength Training
Cardio
Aerobic
Yoga
Stretching
Hiking
Dancing
Sports
Other

Activity master is database-driven.

Flow:
AI extraction
→ activity resolution
→ category resolution
→ time/duration normalization
→ MET lookup
→ backend calorie calculation
→ activity log
→ response

Requirements:
- AI must not calculate authoritative calories.
- Backend owns calorie calculation.
- Store activity, category, duration, intensity, distance and calories.
- Support start/end times.
- If duration is derivable from start/end, calculate it.
- If required information is missing, ask clarification.
- Support follow-ups.

Example:
Aaje 7:30 thi 9 sudhi gym ma strength training kari

Example:
Aaje gym ma 1 hour workout karyu. Add 20 min walk also.

Create reusable calculation service and tests.
